Rudra Priye — how to get a public link (free, no Grok upgrade)

This folder is a complete website. You do not need the Grok Publish button.

EASIEST — Netlify Drop (keeps this exact design)
1. Unzip this folder on your computer.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop in a browser.
3. Drag the unzipped folder (the one that contains index.html) onto the page.
4. Wait a few seconds. Netlify gives you a public link like https://random-name.netlify.app
5. Share that link. Sign up free if they ask you to keep the site.

GOOGLE SITES (if school wants sites.google.com)
1. Open https://sites.google.com and sign in with your school Google account.
2. Create a blank site. Name it e.g. aboutmerudrapriye
3. Insert → Embed → By URL, paste your Netlify link from above.
   OR copy the text from index.html into pages: Home, About, PCM, Court, Training, Record.
4. Click Publish. Google gives you:
   https://sites.google.com/view/aboutmerudrapriye/home

OTHER FREE OPTIONS
- GitHub Pages (needs a free GitHub account)
- Cloudflare Pages
- Tiiny.host — upload the zip

Do not open index.html only on your computer and send that file path.
That is not public. Upload the folder first, then share the https link.
